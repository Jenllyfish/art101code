Fish, Birds, & Butterflies, Oh My!

Utilizing the flocking and sine wave algorithims, I coded a little pond, sky, and garden! I've always loved nature, and was inspired by how professional animators created schools of fish in movies and TV. Growing up, I always watched Pixar movies and animal documentaries, so learning the techniques behind the films was incredibly interesting. However, I do wish that I had more time to create the assets for my algorthim. Due to time constraints, I had to settle on stock photos for my project. Nevertheless, in the future I want to learn how to utilize the flocking algorthim within Blender to create a dynamic 3D environment.
