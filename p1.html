Exercise 1

With this assignment, I wanted to be ambitious and try coding beyond the basic drawing functions I had learned so far. While coding JimBob (blue hair) I felt as if he needed a friend, so I then created TimTam, his twin sister. Both clowns are mostly comprised of ellipses, however, I challenged myself with attempting to wrangle triangles for their mouths. I've always liked creating little characters, and hope to use JimBob and TimTam in a different project of mine in the future. They are extremely silly, and bring me such joy.
TimTam was created in accordance with the extra credit mentioned on the Art 101 website.

Excercise 2

With this assignsment, I aimed to include my personal artwork within the code, since I spent so long illustrating the assets in Photoshop. Project One

For Project 1, I aimed to make a drawing program focused mostly on splatter brushes combined with a single line brush for lineart. Since my art style is very painterly, I stuck with establishing shadows, midtones, and highlights with my brushes to easily mimic how I would draw in Photoshop. Additonally, I wanted to use a vibrant color palette, since I usually use vibrant hues within my personal illustrations.
Overall, I found that the most challenging part of creating the brushes was assigning them the exact color I wanted, since the random() command I used messed with the hues of the orignal palette. It was also quite challenging creating art with a mouse, since I am so accustom to my Huion tablet. Since my tablet did not work as well with the website, I had to attempt clean linework with my mouse, of which had varying success.
